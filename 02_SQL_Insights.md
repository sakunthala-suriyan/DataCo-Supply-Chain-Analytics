\# SQL Business Insights – DataCo Supply Chain Analytics



\## 1. Shipping Performance Analysis

\- Compared actual shipping days with scheduled shipping days.

\- Calculated average shipping delay.

\- Identified differences in shipping performance across categories or shipping modes.



\## 2. Late Delivery Risk Analysis

\- Used a Common Table Expression (CTE) to analyze delivery risk by customer state.

\- Calculated total orders and high-risk orders.

\- Ranked states based on late delivery risk percentage.



\## 3. Category Profitability Analysis

\- Calculated total benefit generated by product categories.

\- Applied the RANK() window function to identify top-performing categories.

\- Compared category-level profitability.



\## 4. Delivery Status Analysis

\- Analyzed order distribution across delivery statuses.

\- Calculated the percentage contribution of each delivery status.

\- Identified delivery patterns across the dataset.



\## 5. SQL Concepts Demonstrated

\- SELECT and WHERE

\- GROUP BY and ORDER BY

\- Aggregate functions: COUNT(), SUM(), AVG()

\- CASE WHEN statements

\- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)

\- Window functions: RANK() and OVER()

\- Type casting

\- Data filtering and sorting



\## 6. Business Recommendations

\- Monitor delivery delays regularly.

\- Investigate states with higher late delivery risk.

\- Improve shipping schedules.

\- Focus on categories with lower profitability.

\- Track delivery status distribution to improve logistics operations.
