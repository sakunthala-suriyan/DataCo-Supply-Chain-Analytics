\# DataCo Supply Chain Performance \& Logistics Analytics



\## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations, delivery performance, logistics efficiency, and profitability using the DataCo Smart Supply Chain dataset.



The goal is to identify delivery delays, evaluate order fulfillment, analyze category-wise profitability, and generate actionable business insights.



\## 2. Project Objectives

\- Analyze overall order performance.

\- Measure on-time and late delivery rates.

\- Identify stores with high late delivery risk.

\- Evaluate profit and benefit across product categories.

\- Analyze shipping performance and fulfillment status.

\- Support data-driven business decisions.



\## 3. Tools \& Technologies

\- Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization.

\- SQL – Data querying and analysis.

\- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.

\- DAX – KPI calculations and measures.

\- Excel / CSV – Dataset handling.



\## 4. Key Performance Indicators

\- Total Orders

\- On-Time Rate

\- Average Delay

\- Late Delivery Rate

\- Total Benefit

\- Average Shipping Days



\## 5. Dashboard Features

\- Delivery Performance Trend

\- Profit by Category

\- Fulfillment Status Mix

\- Order Fulfillment by Store

\- Late Delivery Risk by Store

\- Interactive filters for Shipping Mode, Order Date, and Customer State

\- Key Business Insights



\## 6. Business Value

This analysis helps businesses:

\- Improve delivery performance.

\- Reduce late shipments.

\- Identify high-risk stores.

\- Understand category-wise profitability.

\- Improve logistics and fulfillment decisions.



\## 7. Project Outcome

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor supply chain performance, evaluate logistics operations, and communicate actionable business insights.
