\# Key Business Insights – DataCo Supply Chain Analytics



\## 1. Delivery Performance

\- On-time delivery performance is an important indicator of logistics efficiency.

\- Monitor the monthly delivery trend to identify performance fluctuations.

\- Analyze advance, on-time, and late shipping categories.



\## 2. Shipping and Fulfillment

\- Compare fulfillment performance across different stores.

\- Identify stores with higher late delivery risk.

\- Evaluate shipping modes to understand delivery efficiency.



\## 3. Category Profitability

\- Compare profit contributions across product categories.

\- Identify categories generating higher business benefits.

\- Use category-level analysis to support business decisions.



\## 4. Operational Improvement Opportunities

\- Monitor stores with frequent delivery delays.

\- Improve shipment scheduling and logistics coordination.

\- Track delivery KPIs regularly.

\- Use interactive filters to investigate performance by state, date, and shipping mode.



\## 5. Overall Business Recommendation

Focus on reducing late deliveries, improving fulfillment efficiency, monitoring store-level risks, and strengthening category profitability through data-driven decisions.
